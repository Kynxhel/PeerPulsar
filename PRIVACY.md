# Privacy Policy — Atarca

**Last Updated:** November 2026

**Atarca** is an open-source, peer-to-peer Minecraft Fabric mod. We respect your privacy and do not require any accounts or operate any central data-collection servers.

## 1. Peer-to-Peer Networking
- To establish direct P2P or NAT-traversal connections, Atarca may query public STUN servers (`stun.l.google.com`, `stun.cloudflare.com`), a public signaling channel (`ntfy.sh`), or an open-source TCP relay (`bore.pub`) depending on the connection mode you select.
- No personal data, accounts, Minecraft credentials, or world files are collected by the mod author.

## 2. Contact
For questions or privacy inquiries, please open an issue on the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Kynxhel/Atarca/issues).
